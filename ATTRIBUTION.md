# Attribution

Materials in this repository come from the **CIVICA Data Science Summer School / Hertie School Data Lab (2021)** and the instructors who taught each module. This repo is Juan David Prada’s curated learning portfolio: organization, documentation, and the `showcase/` case study are portfolio packaging around those course materials.

| Module | Upstream / instructors (as preserved in materials) |
